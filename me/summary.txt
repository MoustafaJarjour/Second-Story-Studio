Second Story Studio is a fictional creative reuse business based in Beirut, Lebanon, with the tagline "Keep the story. Change the object." Its mission is to help people preserve the memories attached to their belongings while giving those belongings a useful next chapter through thoughtful repair and redesign. It serves individuals and families with sentimental objects, as well as small cafes and independent shops that want to refresh existing furniture.

The studio offers four services: Furniture Revival for refinishing, cosmetic repair, and creative redesign of small wooden furniture; Fabric-to-Keepsake for turning suitable textiles into cushion covers, tote bags, or decorative patchwork panels; a 20-minute Second Story Consultation costing USD 25, credited toward an approved project; and two-hour Repair & Reimagine Workshops costing USD 35 per person, including practice materials. Furniture and textile projects are quoted individually after assessment. Workshop dates, availability, and project completion times must be confirmed by the team.

The fictional team consists of Maya Haddad, founder and furniture designer; Rami Nassar, textile designer and workshop lead; and Nour Salim, customer experience coordinator. The studio's unique value proposition is to build each redesign around a Preservation Brief describing what the customer wants to keep. Completed projects include a Story Card documenting the original object and its transformation, with a personal story only if the customer chooses to share one.

Consultations and visits are by appointment, Tuesday through Saturday, 10:00-18:00 Beirut time; the team provides the exact address when confirming a visit. Customers interested in follow-up can leave their name, email address, and project notes. General questions do not require contact details. Work begins only after the customer approves the team's proposal and quotation. The studio does not offer electrical repairs, structural safety certification, or specialist conservation of valuable antiques. Transport arrangements, warranty terms, refund policies, and response-time guarantees are not published. Inquiries record interest without confirming bookings or purchases, and feedback or unanswered questions can be recorded for team review. All business details in this summary are invented for the assignment.
